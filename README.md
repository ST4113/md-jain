# M D Jain & Associates — Forensic & Fraud Advisory Website

A custom-developed client website for **M D Jain & Associates**, a specialist Chartered Accountancy practice focused on forensic accounting, fraud investigation and evidence-led advisory work.

🌐 **Live website:** https://mdjain.in/

## About the Client

M D Jain & Associates is a specialist forensic and investigative practice. The firm works on complex and sensitive matters involving financial irregularities, suspected fraud, fund diversion, employee misconduct, data leakage and transaction analysis.

Its work combines forensic accounting, data analytics, digital evidence review, open-source intelligence (OSINT) and field investigation. The practice places emphasis on confidentiality, independence, accuracy and professional judgement.

### Leadership

The firm is led by **CA Mohit Jain**, Founder & Lead — Forensic Accounting and Fraud Investigation.

The website identifies the following professional credentials:

- Chartered Accountant (CA)
- Certified Fraud Examiner (CFE)

## Areas of Expertise

The firm's specialist practice includes:

- Forensic accounting and fraud investigation
- Financial analysis and transaction analysis
- Digital forensic support and digital evidence review
- Data analytics and investigative analysis
- Due diligence assignments
- Fund-flow and financial irregularity investigations
- Dispute-related financial analysis

## Website Overview

The website presents the firm's specialist positioning, leadership, professional credentials, approach and advisory services. It is structured to help prospective clients understand the firm's capabilities and initiate a confidential enquiry.

### Main Website Sections

- **Home** — Introduction to the firm's forensic and investigative practice
- **About** — Firm background, leadership, credentials, philosophy and recognition
- **Services** — Specialist advisory and investigative services
- **Industries** — Industry coverage
- **Approach** — The firm's working methodology
- **Insights** — Professional insights and information
- **Contact Us** — Enquiry channel for prospective clients

### Key Website Elements

- Responsive navigation with a mobile menu

- Service and industry information architecture

## Practice Principles Presented on the Website

1. **Confidentiality** — Restricting access to sensitive engagement information.
2. **Independence** — Reporting findings in line with the available evidence.
3. **Accuracy** — Testing and reconciling figures against source records.
4. **Professional judgement** — Matching investigative methods to the matter.
